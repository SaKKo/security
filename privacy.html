Privacy Notice

What this site stores, what it sends to Google if you allow it, and your rights under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and the EU GDPR.

No account, no forms that send data. Your language and reading progress stay in your browser. Google Analytics runs only if you click "Accept", and you can withdraw that from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page.

Analytics events are not linked to your name or email; the site never asks for them. The marketing category exists in the cookie settings for the future and is not used today.

Sharing and transfers abroad

Google (Google LLC / Google Ireland) processes analytics data on our behalf, on servers that may be outside Thailand, including the United States, under Google’s data processing terms and standard contractual clauses. We don’t sell data or share it with anyone else.

How long

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics property, no longer than 14 months. Browser storage stays until you clear it. Withdrawing consent deletes the _ga cookies from your browser.

Your rights

  • withdraw consent at any time: open cookie settings
  • access, get a copy of, correct or delete your data
  • object to or restrict processing, and data portability
  • complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (pdpc.or.th), or your local data protection authority

Email saklism@gmail.com to use these rights. Because analytics data isn’t tied to your identity, we may need the analytics client ID from your _ga cookie to find it.
